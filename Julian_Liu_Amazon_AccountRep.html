RiQian Motor Debate: Diversification (Negative Side)

Standing for Innovation, Future Market, and Long-term Survival

1. Opening Statement (立论)

Honorable judges, specialization is a trap when the industry's foundations are shifting. RiQian Motor must embrace diversification into new energy motors to ensure its existence in 2026 and beyond.

I. Policy Alignment: Since 2012, the State Council has issued policies for high-efficiency, energy-saving motors[cite: 137]. Staying only in traditional motors is betting against national development plans[cite: 142].
II. Client Retention: Major clients like Dongfeng are already moving toward electric mining trucks[cite: 149]. If we do not provide ancillary motors for new machinery, we will be replaced by competitors who can[cite: 148].
III. First-Mover Advantage: We are already the first manufacturer in China to produce large-tonnage new energy mining truck motors[cite: 150]. Our technology is an industry leader; abandoning this advantage now for short-term profits is strategic suicide[cite: 150, 151].

2. Trapping the Affirmative (进攻提问)

Q1: "Given that infrastructure investment in China is cooling down[cite: 129], how can the Affirmative guarantee growth by staying in a shrinking domestic niche?"
Goal: Force them to admit the traditional market is reaching its ceiling.
Q2: "If core clients like XCMG and Sany fully transition to green machinery, how will your 'specialization' prevent RiQian from becoming completely irrelevant to their supply chains?"
Goal: Highlight the risk of losing major client trust.
Q3: "The Affirmative worries about 2018 losses[cite: 152]. But isn't it true that refusing to innovate now creates a much larger 'opportunity cost' that will be impossible to recover once competitors dominate the green market?"
Goal: Frame 'doing nothing' as the biggest gamble.

3. Defensive Rebuttals (防守反击)

If they say: "Diversification weakens our traditional market position[cite: 162]."

Your Response: "Diversification complements our position. We use our existing channel resources and client relationships [cite: 161] to build a co-creation model. We aren't leaving the mining industry; we are evolving with it."

If they say: "New energy motors are still loss-making[cite: 152]."

Your Response: "These are strategic R&D investments[cite: 140]. Our sales growth jumped from -21% to 46% in 2016 when we started exploring new ancillary needs[cite: 135]. We must use today's profits to buy tomorrow's security."

If they say: "The technology is 'very different'[cite: 139]."

Your Response: "Our independent R&D has successfully reverse-engineered world-class motors from GE and Caterpillar[cite: 95, 99]. Our history proves our ability to master complex technology. To say we can't adapt is to underestimate our engineers' proven track record[cite: 150]."

4. Closing Statement (总结陈词)

Honorable judges, the Affirmative offers a strategy for 2018; we offer a strategy for the future. Staying specialized in traditional motors protects a fading profit, but diversification protects the company itself.

  • We have the technology: We are already industry leaders in new energy motor tech[cite: 150].
  • We have the clients: Our co-operation with Dongfeng is just the beginning[cite: 149].
  • We have the vision: Transforming exploration into a main growth point is how we avoid the 'death of the niche'[cite: 161].

Survival means adapting to the green wave of 2026. Let RiQian Motor be the leader of that wave, not a memory of the past. Support Diversification. Thank you.
