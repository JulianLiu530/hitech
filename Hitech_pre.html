Difficulties We Hit

Reflection · 1 of 3

1 · Restricted Data Access

Data lived in Excel spreadsheets, but Ben rationed access — mid-sized resellers fear copy-stocking (grey shops cloning catalogs and undercutting).

We had to analyse a business through a half-open window.

2 · Internal Disagreement

Group split on direction: TikTok / RedNote influencer push vs. boring B2B search optimization.

Two weeks lost to circular debate before we forced a data-driven tiebreaker.

3 · Coordination Friction

5 members, 4 internships, 2 cities. Sync windows under 3 hours/week.

Slides drifted in tone and logic across authors — classic patchwork risk.

Naming the obstacle is the first step to solving it.30

How We Addressed Them

Reflection · 2 of 3

Fix 1 · Earn the Access

Signed a mutual NDA, anonymised SKUs in our deck, and let Ben redact sensitive margins before we saw them.

Result: enough Excel data unlocked to build VRIO and Value Chain.

Fix 2 · Audit, Don't Argue

Interviewed 10 real mainland buyers. 0/10 used TikTok to source GPUs. Debate ended in one meeting.

Result: ¥0 wasted on the wrong channel.

Fix 3 · Red-Team Saturdays

One member owned the logic spine. Others rotated as grading professor every Saturday, attacking weak slides.

Result: tone consistent end-to-end.

Pattern: replace opinions with field data as fast as possible.

Each fix maps directly to one difficulty on the previous slide.31

What We Still Can't Solve

Open Q1 · Verification Wall
